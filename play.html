How to play

  1. Point at a piece with your index finger.
  2. Pinch and move your thumb and index finger together to carry it to another piece.
  3. Release to swap the two pieces.

You can also drag pieces with a mouse or touch, or select two pieces with clicks or the keyboard.

No photo uploads. Internet is needed to load the tracking models.
